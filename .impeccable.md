## Design Context

### Users
Current mad.builders members, followed by independent builders who want weekly accountability, peer discovery, and a public record of shipping.

### Brand
Terse, builder-first, warm, and opinionated. Use the existing Madrid identity: Archivo and IBM Plex Mono, deep green, cream, thin rules, and plain language.

### Aesthetic Direction
A compact, project-first product interface inspired by Pioneer's dense scoreboard. Rankings are the primary view, projects lead every row, builders appear as attribution, and the weekly accountability action follows below. Avoid oversized marketing headlines, generic cards, gradients, and arbitrary game points.

### Current Product Ceiling
The pilot stores one active project on each builder profile. Introduce project and project-member tables when builders need multiple projects or shared team ownership.

### Accessibility
Preserve semantic headings, visible focus states, 44px touch targets, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, and a mobile layout that does not depend on hover.
